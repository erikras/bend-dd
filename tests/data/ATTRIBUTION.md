# Vendored test data

`list100.txt`, `list1000.txt` and `list10000.txt` are unmodified copies of
`hands/list100.txt`, `hands/list1000.txt` and `hands/list10000.txt` from [dds-bridge/dds](https://github.com/dds-bridge/dds),
the double dummy solver by Bo Haglund and Søren Hein, distributed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0 (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Each deal has a `PBN` line and a `TABLE` line of 20 numbers: the tricks declarer
takes, by strain (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ NT) and then declarer (N E S W).
